Unreadable folders

"Operation not permitted" is macOS privacy protection, not a file permission. "Not scanned" folders are ones macOS would ask you about one by one, so Hoardmap leaves them out instead. shows how to include them all.

Welcome to Hoardmap

It only looks

Hoardmap reads sizes and nothing else. It cannot delete, move, rename or change a single file, and there is no button anywhere that tries.

When you find something big, double-click it, or select it and press Open in Finder. Finder opens at that spot with the item selected. What you do with it there is your call.

Full Disk Access (optional)

Access is on. Folders macOS keeps private are part of the scan.

Access is off. macOS keeps some folders private: Desktop, Documents, Downloads, iCloud Drive, other apps' data, Music, Photos, Mail, Safari and the Trash. Without access they are skipped, listed as unreadable, and left out of the totals. Everything else works the same.

Hoardmap skips them rather than make macOS ask you about each one. The scan starts when you close this window.
